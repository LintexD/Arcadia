# 06. Environmental & Thermal Simulation

## 1. Overview

A dynamic voxel engine can simulate environmental phenomena beyond rigid mechanics:
- **Thermal Diffusion:** Heat spreading through wood, metal, stone, and air.
- **Combustion & Fire Propagation:** Flammable materials igniting, releasing smoke, charring surfaces, and collapsing.
- **Moisture & Vegetation Dynamics:** Rain moisturizing soil, grass spreading, and moss growing along shadowed, damp stone crevices.

---

## 2. Thermal Diffusion & Heat Conduction

### 2.1 The Heat Equation
$$\frac{\partial T}{\partial t} = \alpha \nabla^2 T + \dot{q}_{\text{gen}}$$
Where $T$ is temperature, $\alpha = \frac{k}{\rho c_p}$ is thermal diffusivity, and $\dot{q}_{\text{gen}}$ is heat generated by fire, lava, or explosions.

### 2.2 Discrete Voxel Heat Transfer
For voxel $(x, y, z)$ with material thermal conductivity $k$:
$$\Delta T = \frac{\Delta t}{C_{\text{voxel}}} \sum_{i \in \text{Neighbors}} \frac{2 k_0 k_i}{k_0 + k_i} \cdot \frac{T_i - T_0}{\Delta x}$$
- Metal conduits conduct heat rapidly across tens of voxels per second.
- Stone and wood insulate, localizing heat to surface layers.
- Air convects heat upward along $+Y$.

## 3. Combustion & Scorch Automata

### 3.1 Material Thermal State Machine
Each flammable material (Wood, Foliage, Thatch) defines:
- **Ignition Temperature:** $T_{\text{ignite}}$ (e.g. $300^\circ\text{C}$).
- **Burn Duration:** $t_{\text{burn}}$ (ticks until consumption).
- **Fuel Yield:** Energy emitted during burning.

```text
[ Cold Wood ] ───(T > T_ignite)───► [ Flaming Wood ]
                                           │
                                     (Burn Duration)
                                           ▼
[ Ash / Empty Void ] ◄──(Consumed)── [ Scorched Char ]
```

### 3.2 Visual Integration with Shaders
When a voxel burns:
1. Its material ID transitions from `WOOD_OAK` to `WOOD_CHARRED` or `EMBER_RIM`.
2. The material palette specifies high blackbody emission for `EMBER_RIM`, automatically injecting radiant orange light into the **ReSTIR GI** indirect lighting pipeline.
3. Smoke voxels or particle emitters spawn at the flame boundary, drifting upward into the volumetric fog grid.

---

## 4. Moisture & Procedural Vegetation Growth

Simulating moss and foliage expansion in real-time:
1. **Moisture Accumulation:** Voxels in open air accumulate moisture during rain; voxels near water bodies maintain 100% moisture.
2. **Light Exposure Check:** Using the coarse ambient occlusion / sky visibility bitmask:
   - If a stone voxel has high moisture and moderate shadow: transition surface to `MOSSY_STONE`.
   - If exposed to direct sunlight and high moisture: grass grows.
3. **Tick Scheduling:** Environmental growth updates are scheduled on a low-frequency background thread ($1\text{ to }2\text{ Hz}$), consuming **$< 0.1\text{ ms}$** of frame time.
